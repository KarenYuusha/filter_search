==================================================
SKILL: GUARDIAN
==================================================

Category: Shield Skills
Tier: IV
Required Level: Level 110

Cast an aura which gives various buffs to the party members inside it.
Connected players are represented with a yellow line.
Support Skill (Aura)
Limitation: Shield only
MP Cost: 600MP
Aura Radius: 1 + 0.5*Lvl m (Center at the caster)
I will describe Guardian's buffs separately as:
-- Self Buffer (applied on caster, the icon below HP bar has a time duration)
-- Party Buffer (applied on the party members that stand inside the aura)
SELF BUFFER EFFECTS:
Effect: Increase guard rate of the caster. Also increase aggro multiplier, AMPR and generate auto aggro per second depending on the number of connected players.
NumberOfConnectedMembers = Total number of yellow lines connected from the caster = per link(in short)
ShieldRefine = Refinement value of the shield equipped by the caster.
- Amount of +aggro% multiplier increase: +(40 + 2 * Lvl + 2 * ShieldRefine) * (per link) %
NOTES: The aggro increase does not stack with the aggro% stats additively but they do stack multiplicatively.
- Amount of guard recharge increase: 15 + ShieldRefine %
- Amount of ATK% reduction: -(20 - Lvl * 1.5) * (per link) %
- Amount of MATK% reduction: -(30 - Lvl * 2) * (per link) %
- Amount of AMPR increase: (5 + 1 * Lvl) * (per link)
- Amount of auto aggro generation: 50 + (32 + ref + MAX[0, ref - 12]) * (per link) every 3 sec
NOTE: This calculation is already affected by aggro% multiplier from this guardian self buff. The auto aggro generation is activated once every 3 seconds(reset timer when link changes/lost) and is applied to all active base enemies[that has aggro on you or others that receive your link before] in the map and of course it is unaffected by aggro+% equipment. Also, this buff expires first before starting auto-aggro generation. Btw, this auto aggro generation is already based on 1MP = 10 Aggro now.
Duration:
--30 + 10 * Lvl seconds for Lvl1-5
--30 + 10 * Lvl + (Lvl - 5) * 10 seconds for Lvl6-10
PARTY BUFFER EFFECTS:
Effect for others(non-guardian): grant a buff Reduce the damage taken by enemy, Reduce aggro multiplier buff, and gives AMPR
Effect for guardian users: No effects if both guardians that receive aura each other are on the same guardian level. If there guardian that is lower level than other, then lower lvl’s self-buff +aggro% increase will be disabled and will receive higher lvl’s party-buff -aggro% reduction. Also lower lvl’s auto-aggro generate/3sec will be set to 50/3sec and get reduced by higher lvl’s aggro reduction multiplier too. [And higher lvl gain additional auto-aggro generates the same amount as that reduction above?].
ShieldRefine = Refinement value of the shield equipped by the caster.
- Amount of damage taken reduced: (20 + 1.5 * Lvl + ShieldRefine) %
NOTES: This damage reduction acts as an additional resistance on the top of other damage mitigation stats. The % reduction applies on all categories of attacks including fractionals except ailments. Doesn’t work for Guardian users.
- Amount of Aggro reduced: -(40 + 2 * Lvl + 2 * ShieldRefine) %
NOTES: The aggro reduction does not stack with the aggro% stats additively but they do stack multiplicatively. May won’t work for Guardian users depending on Guardian lvl.
- Amount of AMPR increase: 5 + 1 * Lvl
NOTES: AMPR amount is indeed doubled for DW. Doesn’t work for Guardian users.
Duration: Ends as soon as you are not connected/move out from the caster's aura or duration ends on the caster.

SKILL: DUAL SHIELD
==================================================

Category: Shield Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 50

T3; Shield Only
MP Cost: 300
Increase your Auto Attacks Skill Multiplier by +([0.1 * Skill Level] + BaseVIT/500).
Auto Attacks Hit Count: 3 hits; damage calculation is done once, then divided between the hits
Skill Effect:
Changes auto attack motion. If main wp’s original auto attack range was beyond 3m then reduce the auto attack range down to 3m; no change otherwise. Fortunately, Power Wave still works normally during this auto attack motion changing buff.
Disables Manual Guard stance, but will automatically Guard any attacks (Light Armor = Front; Heavy Armor = Any Directions) as long as both shields are present during idle/running or auto attack only.
Dual Shield's “Auto-guard” will consume Guard gauge upon taking damage. Guard gauge consumption of this skill when taking damage will be halved(seem like always Perfect Timed Guard all the time in auto guard huh).
Dual Shield's "Auto-guard" has Guard MP Recovery.
Other than that, the rest(guard ailment resist, etc.) are pretty much the same as normal guard.
Heavy Armor bonus = removes auto attack interval delay and allow automatic Guard from any direction instead of only from the front
You cannot use this skill if you get inflicted with “Armor Break” Ailment or “Guard Power Break”.
Cannot be reactivated while the buff is still active; also cannot be activated while another auto attack motion change buff (Rampage, Twin Storm, Knife Combat, Minstrel Songs) is active
Buff Effect:
ASPD -[150 - (10 * Skill Level) - (2 * Shield Refine Level)]%
Accuracy +[(10 * Skill Level) + Shield DEF]
Buff Duration: [10 + (2 * Skill Level) + Shield Refine Level] sec OR [until you get inflicted with “Armor Break” Ailment or “Guard Power Break”] OR [until you use "Belagerung" Shield Skill]
-Note: using skills/combos disables Dual Shield state temporary.
Game Description: “Your iron will is stronger than a castle wall. Equips two shields and maintains the state of Guard during combat. Heavy armor increases Guard range and removes the interval of normal attacks.”

==================================================

Category: Shield Skills
Tier: V
Required Level: Level 225

T5; Shield Only
MP Cost: 600
Base Skill Multiplier: 4 + 0.35 * Skill Level
Base Skill Constant: 100 + 10 * Skill Level
Enhanced Base Skill Multiplier: 6 + 0.525 * Skill Level
Enhanced Base Skill Constant: 100 + 10 * Skill Level + baseVIT * (1 + floor(Shield Refine Level/5))
Hit Count: 2 hits; damage calculation is done for each hit
Maximum Cast Range: 12m
Hit Range: circular zone of 4m radius around the main target
Skill Effect:
AOE Attack skill that hits twice. Ends Dual Shield buff upon using this skill.
This skill damage is affected by SRD/LRD depend distance, but unaffected by Long Range passive
When heavy armor is equipped, Each hit will apply one stack of all debuffs below. If this skill lands 2 hits, then its debuff effect will be doubled[in other word, 2 stacks]. Max stack of this skill enemy can take is up to 2 only. Debuff duration: 15 seconds(refreshable)
• If enemy have this debuff and have crit resistance, then Reduce that enemy’s Crit Resistance by flat (Skill Level * stack) until that enemy has ZERO crit resistance. In another word, this cres debuff won’t make from cres to go under 0 at all aka. won’t go negative cres and no cr bonus against non-cres enemies. So, Actual Cres debuff formula = IF(Cres > 0; MIN[Cres ; Skill Level * stack]; 0) flat cres reduction.
• Your or allies' attack to the enemy in immobile state during debuff, will have its increased damage dealt by +(0.5 * Skill Level * stack)%. This damage dealt effect is calculated additively with Brave Aura and others said effect.
• Also, when you or allies auto attack the enemy in immobile state during debuff, then that auto attack will gain more AMPR by +(2 * Skill Level * stack)
Note: This skill can apply debuff even when this skill misses or gets evaded, however it seems like this skill cannot apply debuff twice(just debuff once as only one of two hits can reach) if this skill doesn’t reach the target at all especially when the target dash or moves away very quickly.
Game Description: “Only available during the Skill: "Dual Shields". Deals AoE damage twice and ends "Dual Shields". Increases damage to an immobile target.
"Immobile" refers to [Flinch], [Tumble] and [Stun]. Applies a debuff to the target when equipped with heavy armor. The debuff lasts for 10 seconds. Reduces Critical resistance depending on the level of Belagerung. Increases the damage dealt and Attack MP Recovery if the target is immobile.”
