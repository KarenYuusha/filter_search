==================================================

Category: Hunter Skills
Tier: Not explicitly specified in source
Required Level: Not explicitly specified in source

Kick the target to knockback it farther. Chance to inflict KNOCKBACK.
Active Skill (Physical) [Single Target]
MP Cost: 100MP
Limitation: No Limit
Action Range: 3m
Skill Constant: 10Lvl
Skill Multiplier:
-- [0.1 Lvl] + BaseSTR/200 for Lvl1 to 5
-- [0.1 * Lvl + 0.1(Lvl - 5)] + BaseSTR/200 for Lvl6 to 10
Chance to inflict KNOCKBACK: (90 + 1 * lvl) %
KNOCKBACK distance: (3 + Floor((Lvl-1)/2))m
-- 3m for Lvl1-2
-- 4m for Lvl3-4
-- 5m for Lvl5-6
-- 6m for Lvl7-8
-- 7m for Lvl9-10
NOTES:
-- For regular bosses (Non mini) KNOCKBACK distance is halved.
-- Enemies resist KNOCKBACK status for distance * 0.1 seconds
--The bonus multiplier from BaseSTR is not affected by STR+ and STR+% equipment.
