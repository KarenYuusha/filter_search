SKILL: BLESS
==================================================

Category: Priest Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 15

Source Details:
Support Skill (Party Buffer)
MP Cost = 100MP
Limitation: No Limit
Bless Level = BLv
Advanced Bless Level = ALv
Healing formula:
-- FlatAmountBless (Available if BLvl = 1+) = 10 + BLvl * 5
-- FlatAmountAdvanced (Available if Alvl = 1+) = 10 + Alvl * 2 (+30 if BLvl = 10)
-- MATKMultiplierBless (Available if BLvl = 2+) = 1 + (BLvl - 2)/4 (Staff Bonus = +1)
-- MATKMultiplierAdvanced (Available if ALvl = 2+) = 1 + (ALvl - 2)/4 (Staff Bonus = +1 ONLY if BLvl = 10)
-- INTMultiplier (Available with Staff equipped and BLvl = 1+) = 5 [+5 Bonus if BLvl = 10 and ALvl > 1]
-- Total Heal Amount per Tick = FlatAmountBless + FlatAmountAdvanced + (MATKMultiplierBless + MatkMultiplierAdvanced) * MATKstat/100 + INTMultiplier * INTstat/100
Number of heal counts:
-- BlessCount (Available if BLvl = 1+) = 1+ Floor((2+BLvl)/3)
-- AdvancedCount (Available if ALvl = 1+) = Floor(ALvl - 1)/2 (+1 Bonus if BLvl = 10)
Total Number of heal counts = BlessCount + AdvancedCount
Total Duration = (BlessCount + AdvancedCount - 1) * 5 seconds
--> As such this skill will heal 1 tick when casted and 1 tick every 5 seconds after the first one until duration ends.
--> Simplified formula for both Bless and Advanced Bless at Lvl10 with staff = 120 + INT * (10/100) + MATK * (8/100) with a duration of 45seconds with 1 tick every 5 seconds + 1 tick when casted = total of 10 ticks
NOTE:
--For every Party Buffer Support skill, skillLvl takes priority when multiple casts at a time instance.

Game Description:
Cast a spell to heal HP of the party members at fixed durations

SKILL: NEMESIS
==================================================

Category: Priest Skills
Tier: IV
Required Level: Level 205

Source Details:
Active Skill (Single target Physical & Magic Wall Aoe on target) with Self Buffer
MP Cost = 200MP
Limitations = OHS / Staff / Knuckle only
Action Range = 8m
Physical First Hit Skill Constant = SkillLvl * 60
Physical First Hit Skill Multiplier = 10 (Shield Bonus = Total STR/100)
Hit count for Physical = 1 hit
Magic Additional Hits Skill Constant = SkillLvl * 30
Magic Additional Hits Skill Multiplier = 5 + Total INT/200
Magic Aoe Radius = 2m (MD Bonus = +2.5m)
Hit count for Magic = 1 hit per stack. *every hit = 0.55 sec
Magic AoE Duration = 0.55 sec every stack or until you use Nemesis
Element for both = Light; gain Dual Element with MD if have Nemesis Buff
Buff Effect:
When using this skill without Nemesis Buff, this skill will give you Nemesis Buff and Link Buff first before dealing physical damage. But when using this skill with Nemesis buff, it will release magic wall AoE additional hits(number of hits depending on stack) on target after dealing physical damage and Nemesis buff will end.
Link Buff = a divine retribution link between you and the linked target(that receives your Nemesis damage when you don’t have buff). This buff allows you to gain a stack which can be used for magic additional hits. You can gain 1 stack everytime deal damage to the linked target or receive damage from the linked target. Max stack you can achieve is up to 20. Link will be lost when you're too far(aka. 16m above from the target).
Link Buff Duration = (10 * SkillLvl) sec (Shield Bonus = Duration doubled) {or} until you use Nemesis {or} receive dmg with Silence {or} Link lost due to too far
Nemesis Buff = a buff that will make Exorcism can inflict Dizzy/Dazzled/Fear to the non-Dark target; and the Holy Book can activate Holy Light on Light target. Also, gives Dual Ele MD attribute for both nemesis physical and magic.
Nemesis Buff Duration = (10 * SkillLvl) sec (Shield Bonus = Duration doubled) {or} until you use Nemesis {or} receive dmg with Silence
Note:
Nemesis First Hit, uses pure physical mechanics aka.. uses atk as dmg, physical crit dmg/rate, phys proration, def/pres reduction, phys stab. Meanwhile vice versa for Nemesis Additional Hits which uses pure magic mechanics.
About the Total Str part on Nemesis Physical dmg calc, you need to divide it by 5 first, then round down, then multiply back by 5 again, and there goes this every 5 str = 0.05 instead of going every 1 str = 0.01
Magic Additional Hits, doesn’t inflict magic prorate on target.
Combo tags can affect Magic Additional hits.
Srd, Lrd, Long Range(Shot tree), Unsheathe% doesn’t affect this skill damage
Nemesis Physical First Hit is locked target, since the target on outside of nemesis first hit mini-finale area, this skill still can hit target at all.
Tips: Never use Nemesis again when there is wall animation in the present. If you use Nemesis, then it will make Magic Wall AoE disappear.
If you use this skill without Nemesis buff on target and get miss, then it won’t give Nemesis and Link Buff. However if evaded, still give Nemesis and Link Buff.
Regarding Link buff, that deals damage to the linked target to gain stack.. every skill or action that has multiple hits(even Decoy) still counts as 1 stack. So, you must do multiple actions to gain multiple stacks. Here, which action that can be performed together with your action(auto attack or skills) = Advanced Familia, Holy Book, Enchant Spell, Additional Melee, Additional Magic, Guardian Beam, Imperial Ray, Enchant Burst. On other side, Aggravate and Hidden Arm doesn’t add stack bruh.
Even if you have ailres% from gear/equip or guard.... nemesis/link buff still disappear when receiving damage with Silence. I got this even though I didn’t get Silence, yeah because ailres(not really 100% ailres though).
This skill can get cr bonus from Meikyo for the entire duration if you use this skill with meikyo buff before or even worked during meikyo buff.
Once a critical hit occurs during the repeated magic damage of this skill, all subsequent damage will also be critical.

Game Description:
Deals single target physical damage on the target, and makes a link between you and target. The effect will stack and when activating this skill again, a magic AoE will be added according to the number of stacks after dealing physical.

SKILL: SACRED TEACHINGS
==================================================

Category: Priest Skills
Tier: V
Required Level: Level 285

Source Details:
[Passive] All weapons
Passive Effect:
Allows you and your teammates in your party to gain Temporary HP whenever you or they have full hp and receive excess healing from you. It will be displayed as charge% below the hp bar. That Charge% represents the percentage of how full the temporary HP bank is.
You can only have TemporaryHP up to (1000 * Skill Level)
Temporary HP charge cap = (99.999 - char max HP)/(slv×1000) × 100% [This cap, max at 100%]
Note: this TemporaryHP is not affected by Total HP Fractional damage; but affected by Current HP fractional damage. TemporaryHP can absorb pretty much all damage such as Burn/Poison, Tenacity, even absorb skills that cost hp such as Quick Aura and Saber Aura, etc.
You will gain ATK & MATK by (20 * Skill Level * Charge%)
You can get TemporaryHP from following skills:
Royal Heal;
Mini Heal;
Heal;
Microheal;
Knight Remedy
Below are those skill that cannot give:
Buster Blade; Breathwork; Shining Cross; Crescent Saber; Bouncing Blade; Perfect Defense; Holy Light; Bloody Bite; Nature Wonders; Healing shot; Multiple hunt (shield ver.); Element starter passive; Bloodsucker combo; Remedial rampage Registlet;
Extra Recovery; etc.
*Death Truffle variations -100% Total HP effect does not include temporary HP, so with this skill you can survive consuming death truffle.
( * ) If the total HP (MaxHP + temporary HP) exceeds the 99,999 HP limit, the number of temporary HP gained will be reduced.

Game Description:
Grants temporary HP exceeding your MaxHP when recovering with a Heal-type skill ( * ). ATK and MATK will slightly increase according to the value of temporary HP gained.
