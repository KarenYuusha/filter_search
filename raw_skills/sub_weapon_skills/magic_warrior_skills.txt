For now about the balance ATK MATK formula, I'll use A and B term, A is higher than B. If Atk>Matk, then A is Atk and B is Matk, and vice versa for Matk>Atk.
(Final A > Final B formula) = (Final ATK > Final MATK formula) = (Final MATK > Final ATK formula)
Here complete formula
+B hidden value = MAX[0 ; (Final A - Final B) * (100% - Total negative B%) * SkillModifier% - Final B * Total negative B%]
Note:
If you have -19 Atk% or Matk%, then total negative B% = 19%
SkillModifier% = MIN[100% ; (Skill Level * "Number of Magic Blade skills learned")%]
Max Number of Magic Blade skills can be learned is up to 15 skills instead of 10 skills
[This probably due to when we already learn Dual Bringer, it is already at least 5 skills learned to begin.. so desc that say max 10 skills, then add 5(mw mastery + conversion + reso + espell + db) = max 15 skills learned. But the thing is Skill Modifier% cap to 100% so with Dual Bringer lv 10 then just need at least 10 skills learned to hit cap anyway]
tldr, At level 10 plus +10 Magic Blade Skills learned and without having negative atk%/matk% from any source, then using this skill will make you get following two option below:
If your Final MATK > your Final ATK, then your final ATK will become same amount as final MATK
If your Final ATK > your Final MATK, then your final MATK will become same amount as final ATK
Important Note:
Just make sure you don't have negative atk%/matk% from any source at all cost, since positive atk%/matk% from any source cannot nullify that negative atk%/matk%! Or else like this formula at level 10 plus +10 magic blade skills learned if you somehow have negative%.
MAX[0 ; (Final A - Final B) * (100% - total negative B%) - Final B * total negative B%] = +B hidden value
Example: If you have -10% MATK, and have +15% MATK.
Then let’s say you have 1000 Final ATK & 500 Final MATK, so here you go:
(1000 - 500) * (100% - 10%) - 500 * 10% = 500 * 90% - 50 = 450 - 50 = 400 Hidden MATK gain from this skill
Making it like 1000 Final ATK and 900 Final MATK after using this skill !!
+15% MATK still doesn’t negate/nullify that -10% MATK into +5% MATK. Hence, that’s why you should avoid having negative atk%/matk% at all !!!
Note: if you somehow got a negative result with the calculation above due to having too many negative atk%/matk% or not enough final matk/atk, then the result will be always set to 0 value aka no balance atk/matk effect at all, or id say nothing happened other than crit effect kekw.
