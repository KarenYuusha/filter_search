SKILL: CORROSIVE POISON
==================================================

Category: Assassin Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: Level 35

Source Details:
T2; Dagger/Scroll/BH only
Passive Effect:
Allow you to stack poison multiple times up to +2 with Venom Injection only. Note: You can inflict other poison such as Poison Dagger/Draconic Charge/etc. for initial/first poison(+0), but only Venom Injection can stack poison on top(poison+0 into +1 then +1 into +2)!!
Their chance are dependant on this skill level:
Poison+1 chance is (25 + Skill Level * 5)% of Final Poison+0 Chance(Venom Inject).
Poison+2 chance is (Skill Level * 5)% of Final Poison+0 Chance(Venom Inject).
About Poison duration… it will be extended by +10s everytime you successfully inflict Poison+1 or Poison+2.
If a target has Poison+1 or Poison+2, then attacking that target will automatically inflict poison damage(even other players/merc/pets can inflict poison damage). But this poison can only be inflicted/triggered once at max every 1 second. This means, doing multi hit skill won’t trigger this poison multiple times in 1 second, even if you and your allies hit at same time still trigger once! Note: altho, it is possible to get multiple poison in 1 second. But only achievable with every time enemies attack!
FYI, About poison damage from this skill..
Poison dmg = dex scale + (Atk+matk) scale
Poison+1 dmg = dex scale + 2 * (Atk+matk) scale
Poison+2 dmg = dex scale + 3 * (Atk+matk) scale
You can check full meth in #ailment-information

Game Description:
“Enables the stacking of poison with Venom Injection (max +2). If it turns into enhanced poison due to the stacking, the player's attack will also be able to inflict poison damage.”

Deadly Poison 2 = +50% Physical pierce

Deadly Poison 0 debuff = 10 tick of deadly poison damage starting from first tick 1% damage of (Death Reception Damage to Main Target) then next tick increase by +1% damage, then repeat to next tick until last tick 10% damage....

So… using Arcane Strike on Poison+0 = Explode damage is 20% damage of “Death Reception Damage to Main Target”. Then on Poison+1 = 40% dmg of Death Reception and lastly, Poison+2 = 60% dmg of Death Reception.
Also, Arcane Strike MP Cost on Deadly Poison debuff will be based on Deadly Poison stack. So, using Arcane Strike on Deadly Poison 1, will have its half mp cost. And Arcane Strike on Deadly Poison 2, will have no mp cost. Note: the damage of Arcane Strike still intact as if before this mp reduction or id say same as when all mp used already.
