SKILL: ETERNAL NIGHTMARE
==================================================

Category: Dark Energy Skills
Tier: Not explicitly specified in source
Required Level: Not explicitly specified in source

Support Skill [Self Buffer]
Lvl 4 Skill; No limit
MP Cost: 300 MP
Buff effect:
Eternal Nightmare's passive buff enhances Dark Resistance (+5%) but lowers Light Resistance (-5%)
Increase MaxHP% by 2% * SkillLvL.
While the buff is active you will start losing 1% MaxHP every 2 sec (Minimum 100; meaning if you have below 10k hp, always 100 hp lost). The effect ends when the player reaches 100 hp
Eternal Nightmare's DEF/MDEF reduction effect is affected by Total Dark Power Skills Level, which means: Total Dark Skills points * Eternal Nightmare's LvL * 0.5
Enhances Dark Skill Tree below Tier 3(for more detail check below)
Buff duration: 5min
Notes:
During this skill buff, you cannot regen HP over time(Natural HP regen), except MP
Party members also enjoy boss' reduced DEF/MDEF, but won't suffer HP draining effect, and also no maxhp%, dark resistance, and light resistance
Eternal Nightmare reduces DEF/MDEF before physical pierce%/magic pierce%.
Also, Reduces boss' DEF/MDEF before boss difficulty def bonus apply, so it is like this:
Normal: 50% DEF/MDEF reduction
Hard: 25% DEF/MDEF reduction
Nightmare: 12,5% DEF/MDEF reduction
Ultimate: 8,33% DEF/MDEF reduction.
Eternal Nightmare(Enm) enhances Dark Skills tier 1-3:
Bloody Bite HP recovery limit increased by (10% * Enm Lvl).
Dark Stinger = Additional Skill Multiplier(if +10k current hp) formula got enhanced
+[(CurrentHP) * 0.1 - 1000]/(3000 - 100 * Enm Lvl)
Total Red Tear's hits are increased.
(1 + Enm Lvl * 0.3) * Total Red Tear Hits
So, both(Enm & Red tear) at lv 10 = 20 hits
Sacrifice's stability is increased by (Enm Lvl)%
Demon Claw's Multi Formula got enhanced into
Floor((100 - CurrentHP% + Enm Lvl)/10) * 10 [Maximum 90]
When your currentHP% reach 20%, then demon claw will deal max damage
Regretless' regret effect now has a slight chance to be nullified (1% * Enm Lvl)
