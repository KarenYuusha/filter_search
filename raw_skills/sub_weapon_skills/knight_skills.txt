Tier: Not explicitly specified in source
Required Level: Not explicitly specified in source

Activate Perfect Defense to reduce the damage taken to 0 and heal yourself.
Support Skill (Buff)

---You can block multiple attacks in 1 cast of P.Def but can only heal once per cast.
---Additional aggro added on the top of MP cost base.
---Additional aggro unaffected by aggro+% equipment but base aggro from MP cost still affected by aggro+%
---Using Asobimo Aggro now, 1MP = 10 Aggro

SKILL: FARETH
==================================================

Category: Knight Skills
Tier: Not explicitly specified in source
Required Level: Not explicitly specified in source

Staying still increases the effect (5 levels). Enhances Attack Speed, Physical Pierce, Normal Attack Power, Fractional Damage Reduction. VIT affects Attack Speed and Physical Pierce.
Self buffer skill (Active)
Limitation: Shield only
MP cost: 500
Action range: infinite
Effect: Increase Fractional Damage Reduction, Normal attack, Aspd and Physical Pierce based on number of stacks. Generate 1 stack every 3 seconds, Max Stack 5. But if you move, then your stack will become 0 and need to stay idle(not moving) in order to gain stack.
- Fractional Damage Reduction Increase = (Skill Level / 2.5 * number of stacks)%
- Normal Attack Multiplier Increase = (5 + 1.5 * Skill Level * number of stacks)%
- Aspd+ Increase = Total VIT * Skill Level / 10 * number of stacks
- Physical Pierce% increase = Floor(Total Vit / 100) * number of stacks
- Duration = 3 min regardless level

SKILL: KNIGHT PLEDGE
==================================================

Category: Knight Skills
Tier: Not explicitly specified in source
Required Level: Not explicitly specified in source

[Active] OHS Only
MP Cost: 500
Skill Effect:
Using this skill will put circle 1m radius in front of you with (45 + 4.5 * Skill Level)% as Pledge percentage(refreshable). Everytime anyone gets hit in the Pledge circle, it will reduce this Pledge percentage by 10%. But if anyone gets knocked back in the Pledge circle, then that circle will vanish immediately.
Buff Effect in Circle: all Effects below will be divided equally when there are multiple people inside circle
Buff Duration: When Pledge% reach 0% OR until anyone get knocked back
Damage Reduction for self: +(remaining Pledge%/10)%
Damage Reduction for party: +(remaining Pledge%)%
Damage Reduction of this skill takes place after Sanctuary and before Stone Barrier.
Knockback reduced for self and party by (15 + 2.25 * Skill Level)% [Caster Shield bonus: This knockback reduction will be doubled]
Damage Dealt% for self and party: MAX[Shield Refine ; (1 + Floor[Skill Level/4.5]) ]%
Note: Damage Dealt% is additive with Brave Aura and Aura Blade. Also, only caster need shield to use this skill, teammates without shield can still get max dmg from shield refine.
Game Description: Creates an area that gives protection with the sword of oaths. Within the area, ATK increases and high damage resistance (decreases with each use) is given except to yourself. Disappears if you get a knockback. The more characters in the area, the weaker the effect will be.
OHS bonus: When getting a knockback within the area, the distance can be reduced greatly, but the effect will end as the sword gets knocked away. Furthermore, ATK boost, knockback and damage reduction effect will be shared if there are multiple characters within the area, making the effect weaker.
Shield bonus: Resistance to knockback increases further. Atk will increase further based on your shield’s refinement value.
Note: ATK is misleading, it is Damage Dealt Bonus% like Brave Aura instead
