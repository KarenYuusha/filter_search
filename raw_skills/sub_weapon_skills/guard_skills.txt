==================================================
SKILL: PHYSICAL GUARD
==================================================

Category: Guard Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 180

Increase Ailment Resistance from Guard when running low on HP. Higher skill level enables the skill to be activated with more HP remaining
Passive Skill
Limitation: None
Hp Condition needed to doubles ailres from guard = (5 * Lvl)% of your MaxHP
- At level 10, Doubles guard’s ailment resistance when your HP is below than 50%

==================================================
SKILL: MIRAGE EVASION
==================================================

Category: Guard Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 180

Evasion becomes usable while charging or casting. The effect of this skill is lost for a few seconds if used. The duration depends on the skill level.
Passive Skill
Limitation: None
Allow you can use evasion during cast/chant time. But it has a cooldown (20 - Skill Level) seconds after you use mirage evasion.
