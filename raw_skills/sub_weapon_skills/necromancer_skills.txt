==================================================

Category: Necromancer Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: None

T1; [Active] All Weapons
MP Cost: 100
Time needed for 1 stack gain:
Staff: 1 second
Other Weapons: (11 - Skill Level) seconds
Maximum Cast Range: Any Distance
Graveyard Radius: 3m
-When you step outside of the graveyard, 1 spirit stack disappears every 20 seconds.
-You can use other necromancer's Graveyard (following their Graveyard and Fine Shovel level).

Attack MP Recovery: 0.1 * Skill Level * Stack% * Character's Total Attack MP Recovery
Grave Digger Ailment Resistance: 0.05 * Skill Level * Stack%

Staff Bonus: Gains 1 Spirit Stack per second regardless of skill level. Attack MP Recovery increases based on the Spirit stacks gained. Gains resistance to status ailments. Both Attack MP Recovery per Spirit stack and resistance to status ailments increases with skill level.

SKILL: PHANTOM MISSILE
==================================================

Category: Necromancer Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: None

T1; [Active] Staff Only
MP Cost: 500
Damage Type: Magic
Base Skill Multiplier (each hit): 1
Base Skill Constant (each hit): 500
Maximum Cast Range: 24m
Hit Count: Spirit Stack(s) consumed
Base Cast Time: 0.5 seconds
This skill uses physical critical rate, but uses magic properties for the rest of damage calculation.
Each time this skill deals a critical hit, it adds +(0.1 * Skill Level) base skill multiplier for all of the following hits. This bonus multiplier can be stacked; which means, max multiplier for 10th hit is 10 multiplier, and max skill multiplier if all hits are combined is 55.
Each of Phantom Missile hits adds Chain Cast stack.
This skill is not affected by short/long range damage; not affected by long range skill; affected by magic critical damage, spell burst, concentrate; uses and deals magic proration (following hits uses the same proration value as first hit).
Game Description: Sends forth evil spirits to attack. Performs a magic attack on the target by consuming spirit stacks (max 10). Consuming more spirit increases it's duration (number of hits), and it's power increases with each critical hit (physical).

-Explosion is triggered by resummon and if the skeleton takes damage; but explosion attack only happens if you are engaging a target.
-This attack is forced to be and affected by short range damage; not affected by Long Range Skill; affected by concentrate; uses and deals normal proration.
Game Description: Uses 3 spirit stacks to summon a skeleton soldier. (max 3 on the field) The soldier performs close-range attack. It explodes and disappears when caught in an AoE. Attack power is based on ATK or MATK, whichever is higher.

SKILL: DEMONIC PACT
==================================================

Category: Necromancer Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 260

T3; [Ex Skill] All Weapons
MP Cost: MIN(ROUNDDOWN(Current MP;-2); 2000)
Damage Type: Physical (Uses ATK+MATK)
Base Cast Time: 2 seconds
-The number of Demon summoned in a party is limited to 1 Demon.
-There is a chance to get stunned when aggro switches to you in a party. Stun Chance: (Character's HP% Missing^2/200)%.
Demon's Attacks (Basic and Beam) Properties:
-Each time your Demon attacks, you pay a certain amount of your HP (based on your Max HP, so Sacred Teachings is not included in the cost calculations, but can be used to pay HP). If you don't have enough HP to pay for the attacks, you will die.
-Demon Beam Activation Rate: (5 + Summon MP Cost/100)%. Each Demon's Basic Attack doubles the chance (max 100%). Each Demon Beam halves the chance (no minimum).
-Maximum Attack Range: 7m.
-Unaffected by buffs: Twin Storm, Knife Combat, Shukuchi, Storm Reaper, Asura Aura.
-Follows main weapon's element.
-Unaffected by character's motion speed.
-Affected by short/long range damage; not affected by whack/concentrate; not affected by Long Range Skill; uses and deals normal proration.
Demon's Basic Attack
Base Skill Multiplier: 1 + Summon MP Cost/2000
Base Skill Constant: Summon MP Cost/2
HP Consumption: MAX(3% * Character's Max HP; 300)
-Basic Attack Modifier such as Mana Thrash regislet, Power Wave Modifier regislet, etc, affects this attack's damage.
Demon Beam
Base Skill Multiplier: 2 + TRUNC(0.009 * Summon MP Cost)
Base Skill Constant: Summon MP Cost
HP Consumption: MAX(12% * Character's Max HP; 1200)
Demonic Pact Settings
Pact Count: Skill Level
Mana Slash: Turns into a magic attack with extended range. [Cost: 1 Pact]
Changes Demon's Attacks Damage Type to Magic Damage. Special traits like ATK+MATK usage and use/deals normal proration remains, but the remaining of damage calculation components like INT DTE, Magic Critical Rate/Damage, Magic Stability, Enemy's MDEF/M.res follows magic damage calculations.
Doubles the Maximum Attack Range.
Dark Strike: Interrupts the enemy and performs a powerful attack. [Cost: 2 Pacts]
Demon will only and always perform "Demon Beam" when the target is interrupted by Flinch/Tumble/Stun.
Hollow Strike: Becomes an attack with the element the enemy is weak to. [Cost: 2 Pacts]
Demon's Basic Attack and Demon Beam becomes always stronger element against the enemy.
Demon King Revelation: Adapts to action speed. [Cost: 4 Pacts]
Demon's motion speed follows Character's motion speed. (Freeze and Paralyzed are excluded)
Substitute Contract: Depending on the situation, you may survive. [Cost: 4 Pacts]
Prevents you from dying if you take high damage or can't pay for Demon's Attacks. Desummons the demon (can't resummon) and restores HP.
HP Restored: Character's Attack MP Recovery * MIN(Total Demon's Attacks During Summon;99)
Activates before Saber Aura, after Magic Skin.
Anchor of Life: HP consumption by the demon will not knock you out. [Cost: 1 Pact]
When you have not enough HP to pay for Demon's Attacks, you are left with 1 HP while the Demon continues to attack the enemy. Everytime the Demon attacks, it's damage Multiplier and Constant drops by 200 initial Summon MP Cost (stops dropping once it is left with 100 Summon MP Cost).
Activates before "Substitute Contract".
Blood Contract: Reduces MP, but reduces Max HP. [Cost: 2 Pacts]
Restores half of Summon MP Cost without reducing Demon's Attacks Damage.
HP Penalty: Max HP -50% (Equivalent to Max HP from equipments)
Demonic Pact Settings: Barrier
Barrier Damage Reduction: (15 + TRUNC(Base VIT/50))%
Demonic Barrier: Reduces damage when nearby. [Cost: 1 Pact]
Barrier Range: 7m from Demon
Only reduces Magical and Physical Damage
Doubles Damage Reduction
Barrier Expansion: Reduces the damage even when far away. [Cost: 2 Pact]
Range: 21m from demon
Only reduces Magical and Physical Damage
Spirit Distortion: Reduces fractional damage when nearby. [Cost: 2 Pacts]
Range: 7m from demon
Only reduces Fractional Damage
Note: Selecting multiple barrier pacts will activate the best effects only.
Example:
Activates Demonic Barrier, Barrier Expansion, Spirit Distortion..
Range: 21m from demon
Reduces Magical, Physical, and Fractional Damage
Doubles Damage Reduction.
Game Description: A contract with a demon. The ability changes based on the amount of MP offered when forming the contract. Each attack by the demon costs the caster some HP. Reusing the skill will terminate the contract. The Demon may betray you if things turn bad (when all party members dies, except for you, you will be killed by the Demon).
