Important notice:
• To utilize the wizard skills(except Familia skill), you must have your Familia present with you.
• Cat Base Cast Time means your Familia is going to cast time wizard skill instead of you. Except Imperial and Mana Crystal which need you to do cast time.
• If your Familia gets hit during mid cast, the cast will fail due to interruption
• User can change the Familia and it's color via ex skills on wizard skills
New Wizard Damage Info
All of Wizard Attack Skill’s damage scales on 75% Matk and 25% Atk. We will call it Wizard ATK. Here how this wizard's damage should be.
Wizard ATK = Floor(MATK * 75%) + Floor(ATK* 25%)
Base Damage(Wizard Skills) = (Wizard ATK + player's level - mob's level) * (100 - mob's Magic Resistance)/100
Only MDEF and Magic Resist% can reduce these damages. Meanwhile DEF and Physical Resist% cannot. These damages are based on Magic Proration.

SKILL: STONE BARRIER
==================================================

Category: Wizard Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 125

Limitation: Staff, MD, MD sub weapon only
Buff skill(active)
MP cost: 200 mp
Action Range: Unlimited
Cat Base Cast Time: (6 - 0.2 * skill level) seconds, unaffected by CSPD
Skill Effect:
- This skill gives shield hp by 500 + (200 * skill level) + (TotalVIT * 2)
- This skill barrier works like an additional hp or shield hp to the user and it's party members. Shield HP can absorb damage, so this way we can make any damage become 1, only if damage received is lower than Shield HP.
- If the damage received is higher than Shield HP, then you will receive more than 1 damage and will get hidden effects that can halve that damage higher than Shield HP. [Example = You have 2240 Shield HP, and you receive 5000 damage. Then Shield HP became 0 because 5000 dmg > 2240 shield hp, and 5000 damage became 2760 damage because 5000 - 2240, then got halved into 1380 damage. Now you will receive 1380 damage and the stone barrier shield buff will end.]
- Ignition & poison damage increase by 100% [applied additively with Serum]
- Skill buff duration = 30 + (3 * skill level) seconds
Game Description: Familiar is told to perform a safeguard magic. It gives protection to reduce certain damage, but the damage from Ignite and Poison increases(drawback). Can't be used repeatedly.
Note:
- You cannot use this skill if you already have this skill buff effect.
- When your teammates already have this skill buff and you use this skill, then this skill only gives shield buff to you and teammates who don't have this skill buff only, and doesn’t replace teammate’s previous shield buff at all.
