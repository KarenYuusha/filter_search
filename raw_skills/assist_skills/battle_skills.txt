==================================================
SKILL: MAGIC UP
==================================================

Category: Battle Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 20

Source Details:
Type: Passive

Description:
Slightly increases MATK.

Effect:
Increases flat MATK based on the player's level.

Formula:
MATK Increase =
PlayerLvl * (2.5 * SkillLvl) / 100

Game Description:
Not provided in source.


==================================================
SKILL: CONCENTRATE
==================================================

Category: Battle Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 20

Source Details:
Type: Passive

Description:
Has an extremely low chance to increase the damage of the last magic attack used.

Effect:
Increases the damage dealt by a magic skill by a set percentage.

Activation Rate:
SkillLvl %

Damage Increase:
(10 + SkillLvl) %

Notes:
- The skill does not activate for placed skills.
- The damage increase is applied additively to the skill's Long Range modifier.

Not provided in source.

==================================================
SKILL: DESPERATE RESIST
==================================================

Category: Battle Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: Level 60

Source Details:
Description:
Reduces damage taken while the player is locked down by ailments such as Tumble and Stun.

Effect:
Reduces damage taken whenever the player is "Action Locked".

Damage Reduction:
SkillLvl %

Action Lock Conditions:
The character is considered Action Locked for a set duration when any of the following ailments are inflicted:
- Flinch
- Tumble
- Stun
- Knockback
- Sleep

Not provided in source.

==================================================
SKILL: SPELL BURST
==================================================

Category: Battle Skills
Tier: IV
Required Level: Level 180

Source Details:
Type: Passive

Description:
Allows magic attacks to critically hit under certain conditions.

Effect:
Provides magic Critical Rate and magic Critical Damage conversion.

Magic Critical Rate Conversion:
(2.5 * SkillLvl)% of total Critical Rate before Critical Resistance is applied.

Magic Critical Damage Conversion:
(2.5 * SkillLvl)% of total Critical Damage gain, excluding the base 100 Critical Damage.

Related Information:
See the Critical Formula section for more details about magic critical hits.

Game Description:
Not provided in source.
