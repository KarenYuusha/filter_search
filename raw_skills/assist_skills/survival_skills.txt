==================================================
SKILL: SAFE REST
==================================================

Category: Survival Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: 1
Type: Passive

Description:
Increases Natural HP Regen while outside combat.

Effect:
When outside battle, increases Natural HP Regen at the end of the regeneration calculation.

Flat Natural HP Regen Increase:
10 * SkillLvl

Natural HP Regen% Increase:
10 * SkillLvl %

Base Natural HP Regen Formula:
BaseNHPR =
MaxHP / 25 * (100% + TotalEquipmentNHPR%) + 10 + TotalEquipmentFlatNHPR

Final Natural HP Regen Formula:
FinalNHPR =
BaseNHPR * (100% + TotalSkillNHPR%) + TotalSkillFlatNHPR

Notes:
- Natural HP Regen from equipment and avatars is included in the base NHPR value.
- Natural HP Regen from skills is applied after base NHPR is calculated.

- Natural MP Regen from skills is applied after base NMPR is calculated.

SKILL: SOBER ANALYSIS
==================================================

Category: Survival Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: 20
Type: Passive

Description:
Regenerates MP both inside and outside combat. The recovery amount is small.

Recovery Interval:
Every 3 seconds.

MP Recovery Amount:
5 * SkillLvl % * BaseNaturalMPRegen

Base Natural MP Regen:
BaseNMPR =
INT(INT(MaxMP / 100) * (1 + TotalNMPR% / 100)) + 1 + TotalFlatNMPR

Notes:

- Total NMPR% excludes Emotion NMPR boosts.
