Category: Support Skills

GENERAL INFORMATION

Tier Requirements:
- Tier I: Level 10
- Tier II: Level 30
- Tier III: Level 70
- Tier IV: Level 150

Shared Rules and Notes:
- Support skills are various party buffing and healing skills used to help support the party.
- All Support Skills except Sanctuary are un-comboable.
- The bottom 2 branches apply a penalty to the caster and disappear if the caster is damaged.
- Positive effects are applied to party members inside the aura, including the caster.
- Aura-based Support Skills generate [30 + 30 * PlayersConnected] aggro every 3 seconds per active aura on enemies that already have base aggro on the caster.
- Aura aggro is unaffected by Aggro+% equipment.
- Source note: using Asobimo aggro values, 1 MP = 10 Aggro.


==================================================
SKILL: FIRST AID
==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 10

Source Details:
Type: Support
Role: Party healer
MP Cost: Variable
Action Range: 2m

Description:
Reduce the revive time for dead party members.

Revive Time Reduction:
30 + 2 * Lvl % of current revive time

MP Cost Details:
- Starts from 1 MP.
- Increases by 1 MP every time First Aid is used consecutively.
- Reduced by 1 MP every 1 minute if no First Aid was given.
- Resets to 1 MP when changing maps.

Notes:
- Click on a dead party member's HP bar or character in the overworld to open the menu for giving First Aid.
- Without this skill, giving First Aid reduces the current revive time by 30%.
- Giving First Aid counts as party-buffer aggro generation.

Game Description:
"First Aid" effect in Support menu increases. The waiting time to revive is largely reduced.

Mini Heal will target the caster automatically if no target is specified.

SKILL: RECOVERY
==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: Level 30

Source Details:
Type: Support
Role: Party/Self healer
MP Cost: 100
Action Range: 48m (For healing party members only)

Description:
Cast a spell to cure ailments. If the target has no ailment, grant a buff that prevents one ailment for a fixed duration.

Effect:
Heal an ailment if one is present, or give a buff that prevents one ailment.

MP Recovery:
(Lvl * Lvl) MP if an ailment is successfully removed or the prevention buff successfully prevents an ailment.

Buff Duration:
10 + 2 * Lvl seconds

Notes:
- Click on a party member's HP bar or overworld character to use the skill on them.
- If used on self, it acts as a self-buffer aggro generator.
- If used on a party member, it acts as a party-buffer aggro generator.
- It is currently unknown which ailment receives priority when the target has more than one ailment.
- This skill cannot heal or prevent Flinch, Tumble, Stun, or Knockback.
- It also cannot prevent Magic Flinch caused by being hit while casting magic.

Game Description:
Remove a target's one status ailment. If there is no ailment to remove, it becomes a buff to remove one ailment at once for a few seconds. Restore the user's MP a little when succeeding in removing an ailment.
Recovery will target the caster automatically if no target is specified.

SKILL: SANCTUARY
==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 70

Source Details:
Type: Support
Role: Placed support
MP Cost: 400

Description:
Generate a platform that reduces damage taken depending on the character's MaxHP.

Effect:
Place a platform on the ground that reduces damage taken while standing within it.

Condition:
Damage reduction activates only if the incoming damage is lower than a percentage of the character's MaxHP.

Platform Radius:
1 + Lvl / 3 m from the caster's position

Platform Radius at Level 10:
4.33m

MaxHP Damage Threshold:
(5 + 0.5 * Lvl)% of MaxHP

Damage Reduction:
(30 + [(Lvl - 1) / 3] * 20)%

Duration:
5 + Lvl seconds

Notes:
- This skill acts as a party-buffer aggro generator when placed where party members are within the platform radius.
- The damage reduction applies to any category of attack, including fractional damage, as long as the activation condition is satisfied.

Game Description:
Create a sanctuary and protect allies. Reduce damage taken while in the area. The amount depends on each player's MaxHP. Ineffective against fatal damage.

SKILL: LIFE RECOVERY
==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 10

Source Details:
Type: Support
Role: Aura-based party/self buffer
MP Cost: 300

Description:
Cast an aura that continuously restores HP to party members inside its range.

Aura Radius:
5 + Lvl / 2 m from the caster

Effect:
Generate an aura that heals the HP of party members inside the aura.

Caster Penalty:
Increase damage taken.

HP Heal Amount:
10 + Lvl * 4 + TotalVITofCaster

HP Healing Interval:
Every 3 seconds while the buff is active

Damage Taken Increase:
+(400 - Lvl * 20)%

Damage Calculation:
Applied multiplicatively at the end of damage calculation after being added with Magic Barrier bonus and Gem effects.

Duration on Caster:
15 minutes

Notes:
- Aura-based Support Skills are removed if the caster is damaged.
- This includes 0 damage from skills such as Perfect Defense, except Zantei.
- The aura remains active if the caster dodges or evades.
- Positive aura effects apply to party members inside the aura, including the caster.
- The penalty is applied only to the caster.
- Party members receive the effect only while inside the aura radius.

Game Description:
Create an area that regenerates HP. The player's DEF decreases. The effect ends when the player take damage.
