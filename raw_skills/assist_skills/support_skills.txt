Category: Minstrel Skills

GENERAL INFORMATION

Weapon Restrictions:
All Minstrel skills are limited to:
- Two-Handed Sword
- Bow
- Bowgun
- Katana
- Staff
- Magic Device

Buff Range:
24 m

Song Interaction Rules:
- If two players are singing the same song, each singer receives the buff from their own song.
- Party members receive the buff from the higher-level song.
- If the song applies a party-member penalty, the caster receives their own penalized buff instead of another singer's full buff.
- Walking while singing outside combat interrupts the song.
- Walking while singing during combat does not interrupt the song, but Movement Speed is halved.
- Basic attacks and skill casts interrupt singing but do not reset singing progress.
- An interrupted song can be continued by using Ad-Lib before the 9-second timer within the 10-second duration ends.
- Different song skills cannot be sung at the same time.
- Song buffs and Beat Blast stacking affect Guild Raid allies, up to 16 members.

Acoustic Buff:
Continuing a song for a certain amount of time transforms it into an Acoustic Buff.

Acoustic Buff Properties:
- Acoustic Buffs are permanent effects after activation.
- They remain active indefinitely unless the performer is knocked out.
- In boss fights, the source notes that the song may remain active indefinitely even after this condition.
- Party members who are knocked out and then revived receive already-active full buffs.
- Party members who enter the song caster's map also receive already-active full buffs.

Acoustic Buff Progression:
The MP cost and singing duration required to transform the next song into an Acoustic Buff increase as more Acoustic Buffs are active.

Sing Duration:
5 + (AcousticBuffOrder - 1) * 4 seconds

MP Cost:
(AcousticBuffOrder - 1) * 100 MP

EX Skill: Setlist
Allows the player to arrange a setlist and sing multiple songs in sequence from one shortcut button.

Maximum Songs in Setlist:
6

Tier Requirements:
- Tier I: No level requirement
- Tier II: Level 60
- Tier III: Level 120

SKILL: HEALING SONG
==================================================

Category: Minstrel Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: None
Type: Support
Proration: None
Mechanic: Song / Acoustic Buff

MP Cost: 100
Action Range: Unlimited

Description:
A break for travelers. Increases EXP Gain and Natural Regeneration when not in combat. Grants additional HP and MP recovery when battle ends based on battle duration. Cannot be sung at the same time as other songs.

Buff Effects:

EXP Gain Increase:
CasterLvl / 10 % + SkillLvl %

Notes:
- Can be used for boss battles, common monsters, and minibosses.
- Does not affect pet leveling.

Natural HP Regen Increase:
10 * SkillLvl %

Natural MP Regen Increase:
5 * SkillLvl %

HP Recovery After Battle:
3 * SkillLvl * Min(BattleDurationSeconds, 30)

MP Recovery After Battle:
SkillLvl * Min(BattleDurationSeconds, 30)

Battle Recovery Notes:
- Only battle duration while Healing Song's buff is active is counted.
- HP and MP recovered after battle are flat values.

Natural HP Regeneration Formula:

Base NHPR:
MaxHP / 25 * (100% + TotalEquipmentNHPR%) + 10 + TotalEquipmentFlatNHPR

Final NHPR:
BaseNHPR * (100% + TotalSkillNHPR%) + TotalSkillFlatNHPR

Natural MP Regeneration Formula:

Base NMPR:
MaxMP / 100 * (100% + TotalEquipmentNMPR%) + 1 + TotalEquipmentFlatNMPR

Final NMPR:
BaseNMPR * (100% + TotalSkillNMPR%) + TotalSkillFlatNMPR

Notes:
- Natural HP Regen from equipment and avatars is added into the base NHPR calculation.
- Natural HP Regen from skills is applied after base NHPR is calculated.
- Natural MP Regen from equipment and avatars is added into the base NMPR calculation.
- Natural MP Regen from skills is applied after base NMPR is calculated.

SKILL: LIFE SONG
==================================================

Category: Minstrel Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: None
Type: Support
Proration: None
Mechanic: Song / Stack / Acoustic Buff

MP Cost: 200
Action Range: Unlimited
Maximum Stacks: 50

Description:
The beat of life. Reduces the revive timer at fixed intervals and accumulates an effect that mitigates fractional damage. If HP reaches 0, accumulated stacks are converted into HP recovery.

Stack Gain:
- Non-aggro holder: Gains 1 Life Song stack every 4 seconds.
- Aggro holder: Gains 1 Life Song stack every 2 seconds.
- Maximum: 50 stacks.

HP Recovery When HP Reaches 0:
INT(MaxHP / 1000 * SkillLvl * LifeSongStack)

Fractional Damage Taken:
Max(FractionalDamage - LifeSongStack, INT(FractionalDamage% / 2)) %

Equivalent Fractional Damage Reduction:
Min(LifeSongStack, FractionalDamage% / 2) %

Notes:
- Fractional damage reduction works similarly to Heavy Armor Damage Reduction.
- It is calculated after Heavy Armor Damage Reduction.
- Removes Knockback from death.
- Reduces revive time every 3-second interval by:
  3 * SkillLvl seconds
- Life Song stacks reset to 0 whenever the player takes Fractional Damage.
- Life Song stacks also reset to 0 whenever the player's HP reaches 0.

SKILL: AD-LIB
==================================================

Category: Minstrel Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: 60
Type: Support
Proration: None
Mechanic: Song Resume / Defensive

MP Cost: 100
Action Range: Unlimited

Description:
Resumes the most recently used song. Reduces damage taken during Ad-Lib if a song is currently active. At Skill Level 10, this effect becomes invincibility instead of damage reduction.

Damage Reduction:
10% * SkillLvl

At Skill Level 10:
- Damage Reduction reaches 100%.
- Grants invincibility.
- Invincibility duration: 1 second.

Conditions:
- Damage Reduction or invincibility only occurs if a song was being played before Ad-Lib was cast.
- The damage reduction does not stack with Sound Veil.
- When both are applicable, the higher damage reduction value is used.

Interrupted Song Resume Conditions:
1. Being hit by an enemy, using another skill, or performing a basic attack starts a 10-second countdown.
2. Ad-Lib must be used within those 10 seconds.
3. If Ad-Lib is not used within the countdown, the interrupted song disappears completely.

SKILL: SOUND VEIL
==================================================

Category: Minstrel Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: 120
Type: Passive
Mechanic: Song / Acoustic Buff Scaling

Description:
While a song skill is active, the player's Aggro output becomes 1. Reduces all types of damage according to the number of Acoustic Buffs. Damage from enemies directly targeting the player cannot be reduced by this effect.

Damage Reduction:
Min(AcousticBuffCount, 9) * SkillLvl

Aggro Effect:
While a song skill is active, Aggro output becomes 1.

Restrictions:
- Damage from enemies directly targeting the player cannot be reduced by Sound Veil.
- Does not stack with Ad-Lib.
- If both Sound Veil and Ad-Lib apply, the higher damage reduction value is used.

Note:
The source notes that a bug may occur where the damage reduction buff fails to activate.
