Category: Minstrel Skills

GENERAL INFORMATION

IMPORTANT NOTE:
All Minstrel skills are limited to two-handed sword, bow, bow-gun, katana, staff and magic-device.
Buff Range: 24m
If 2 players are singing the same song, they'll get buff from their own song only and the party member will get buff of the song which has a higher level; if the song effect has party members penalty, the caster will get their own penalized buff instead of other singer's full buff.
Walking while singing a song outside combat will interrupt the singing; unlike if it is done in combat (but your movement speed gets halved). Basic attack or casting skill will interrupt the singing, but will not reset singing progress. You can continue singing by using Ad-Lib before the 9 seconds timer (10s duration) ends.
Song skills cannot be sung at the same time with other songs. Song buffs & Beat Blast stacking affect guild raid allies (16 members).
Acoustic Buff
Continuing a song skill for a certain period of time transforms it into an acoustic buff (permanent effect). Once a song skill transforms into an Acoustic Buff, it will remain active indefinitely unless the performer is knocked out (unless in boss fights, somehow the song will be active forever).
Party members who were knocked out then revive, also when they just arrive at the song caster map, will get the full buffs that are already available.
MP cost of song skills and time to transform the next song into an Acoustic Buff increase as more songs become Acoustic Buffs; it is as follow:
Sing Duration: 5 + (Acoustic Buff Order - 1) * 4 seconds
MP Cost: (Acoustic Buff Order - 1) * 100 MP
[Ex Skill] Setlist
You are able to make your setlist arrangement. This enables you to sing multiple songs in order from one shortcut button (Max 6 songs).
Acoustic Buff

Tier Requirements:
- Tier I: None
- Tier II: Level 60
- Tier III: Level 120

SKILL: HEALING SONG
==================================================

Category: Minstrel Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: None

Source Details:
MP cost: 100
Support skill: (none proration)
Action range: Unlimited
Buff Effect:
EXP Gain%: + (Casterlv/10)% + Skill Level%
-Can be used for boss battle, common monster, and miniboss.
-Does not affect pet leveling.
Natural HP Regen%: + 10 * Skill Level
Natural MP Regen%: + 5 * Skill Level
HP Recovery After Battle: + 3 * Skill Level * MIN(Battle Duration[seconds]; 30 seconds)
MP Recovery After Battle: + Skill Level * MIN(Battle Duration[seconds]; 30 seconds)
-Only battle duration with healing song buff counts for these.
-HP and MP healed is flat value.
Note:
-- Natural HP regen from equip & avatar are added as base NHPR value. Meanwhile NHPR from skill is applied after base NHPR is calculated.
Base NHPR = MaxHP/25 * (100% + Total eq NHPR%) + 10 + total eq flat NHPR
Final NHPR = Base NHPR * (100% + Total NHPR% skill) + Total flat NHPR skill
-- Natural MP regen from equip & avatar are added as base NMPR value. Meanwhile NMPR from skill is applied after base NMPR is calculated.
Base NMPR = MaxMP/100 * (100% + Total eq NMPR%) + 1 + Total eq flat NMPR
Final NMPR = Base NMPR * (100% + Total NMPR% skill) + Total flat NMPR skill

Game Description:
A break for travelers. Increases EXP Gain and Natural Regen when not in combat. Grants additional HP and MP recovery when the battle ends, based on the battle duration. Cannot be sung at the same time with other songs.

SKILL: LIFE SONG
==================================================

Category: Minstrel Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: None

Source Details:
MP cost: 200
Support skill: (none proration)
Action range: Unlimited
Buff Effect:
Gains one Life Song stack every 4 seconds (non aggro holder) or 2 seconds (aggro holder) (Max 50 stacks).
HP recovery upon reaching 0 HP:
HP Recovery Amount: INT(Max HP/1000 * Skill Level * Life Song Stack)
Party Fractional Damage Reduction Effect:
Fractional Damage% Taken: MAX((Fractional Damage - Life Song Stack); INT(Fractional Damage%/2))%
or you can say..
Fractional Damage% Reduction: MIN(Life Song Stack; Fractional Damage%/2)%
-The fractional damage reduction is similar to that of Heavy Armor Damage Reduction, but is calculated after Heavy Armor Damage Reduction.
Removes Knockback from death.
Reduces revive time every 3 seconds interval by 3s * Skill Level.
Stack resets to 0 everytime the player takes Fractional Damage or when their HP reaches 0.

Game Description:
The beat of life. Reduces the revive timer at fixed intervals and stacks an effect that mitigates fractional damage. If HP hits 0, the accumulated value turns into a recovery barrier.

SKILL: AD-LIB
==================================================

Category: Minstrel Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: Level 60

Source Details:
MP cost: 100
Support skill: (none proration)
Action range: Unlimited
Buff Effect:
Damage Reduction: 10% * Skill Level (invincible at Skill Level 10; invincible duration = 1s)
-Damage Reduction/Invincible only happens if a song is being played before Ad-lib is casted.
-The damage reduction does not stack with Sound Veil, (chooses whichever reduction is higher).
Resume interrupted song conditions:
i) Player get hit by a mob or use any other skill or do basic attack which starts a 10 seconds countdown.
ii) Ad-lib must be used within those 10 seconds or the interrupted song will vanish completely.

Game Description:
Resumes the song skill used most recently. Reduces damage taken during Ad-lib if a song skill is ongoing. (At Lv 10, it grants invincibility instead of damage reduction.)
