==================================================
SKILL: GOLEM CALL
==================================================

Category: Golem Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: None

Source Details:
Type: Active
Allowed Weapons:
- Bowgun
- Staff
- Main Magic Device

MP Cost: 500
Base Cast Time: 2 seconds
Base Buff Duration: 10 seconds
Base Shield Range: 4 m

Description:
Summons a golem to assist in battle. The golem performs basic attacks and can provide damage reduction through Golem's Shield.

Golem Properties:
The golem uses the character's following stats:
- Attack Speed
- Critical Rate
- Critical Damage
- Pierce
- Stability
- Character Movement Speed

Notes:
- Motion Speed% from character stats is not included in the golem's Attack Speed.
- Golem movement speed is based on the character's default Movement Speed, not movement gained from running or skills.

Golem Attack Properties:
- Element: Weapon Dependent
- Uses normal proration.
- Deals no proration.
- Affected by Short Range Damage and Long Range Damage.
- Not affected by the Long Range skill.
- Not affected by Whack.
- Not affected by Concentrate.
- Affected by Role Model Registlet.
- Not affected by Zero Stance Registlet.

The following buffs do not apply to Golem attacks:
- Twin Storm
- Dual Shield
- Knife Combat
- Shukuchi
- Sliding
- Bouncing Blade
- Storm Reaper
- Magic Arrow from Hunter Skills
- Asura Aura

EX Skill: Golem Selection

The player may choose one of three golem types.

Lancer-Type:
Behavior:
Charges at the target and has high attack power.

Damage Type:
Physical

Damage Stat:
Uses MATK

Base Attack Multiplier:
2

Maximum Attack Range:
1 m

Movement Speed Coefficient:
75%


Shielder-Type:
Behavior:
Stays near the user and attacks targets within range.

Damage Type:
Physical

Damage Stat:
Uses MATK

Base Attack Multiplier:
1

Maximum Attack Range:
7 m

Movement Speed Coefficient:
50%

Base Damage Reduction from Golem's Shield:
10%


Buster-Type:
Behavior:
Has long attack range and deals magic damage.

Damage Type:
Magic

Damage Stat:
Uses MATK

Base Attack Multiplier:
1

Maximum Attack Range:
14 m

Movement Speed Coefficient:
25%


EX Skill: Magic Lamp Power Distribution

Magic Lamp Count:
SkillLvl

Surplus Allocation:
Buff Duration Increase =
10 * SurplusPoint seconds

Effect:
The golem remains active longer when more surplus power is left.

Attack Allocation:
Golem Attack Multiplier Increase =
0.2 * AttackPoint

Golem Accuracy Increase =
20 * AttackPoint

Shield Allocation:
Damage Reduction Increase =
2% * ShieldPoint

Speed Allocation:
Golem Movement Speed Coefficient Increase =
15% * SpeedPoint

Notes:
Attack, Shield, and Speed Magic Lamp allocations gain additional effects from:
- Attack Boost
- Shield Boost
- Speed Boost

Game Description:
Summons a golem to assist in battle. Your attacks will not restore MP. Instead, the golem's normal attacks restore MP. The EX skill allows selection of the golem type and adjustment of the Magic Lamp.

SKILL: MAGIC GRENADE
==================================================

Category: Golem Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: None

Source Details:
Type: Active
Allowed Weapons:
- Bowgun
- Shield
- Main Bare Hand

MP Cost: 100
Element: Weapon Dependent
Damage Type: Magic
Damage Stat: MATK

Description:
Destabilizes the Magic Lamp's power and throws it like a bomb. The skill deals AoE magic damage, but cannot reach targets beyond its maximum throwing distance. INT and TEC increase its power.

Element Note:
If using Bowgun with Arrow, the element can depend on the Arrow.

Base Skill Multiplier:
1 + 0.4 * SkillLvl + Max(BaseINT / 100, BaseTEC / 50)

Base Skill Constant:
100

Maximum Throwing Distance:
3 + 0.5 * SkillLvl meters

Explosion Range:
2 + 0.2 * SkillLvl meters

Game Description:
Destabilizes the magic lamp's power to use it like a bomb. It's an AoE attack, but it won't reach targets that are too far away. INT and TEC will boost its power further.

SKILL: BARRIER SCREEN
==================================================

Category: Golem Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: Level 200

Source Details:
Type: Active
Allowed Weapons:
- Bowgun
- Shield

MP Cost: 300

Description:
Deploys a drone that creates a magic barrier. The barrier reduces the power of certain straight-line, bullet, or bowling blade-wave attacks that pass through it. The barrier reduces damage once and then breaks.

Buff Duration:
Min(60, 12 * SkillLvl) seconds

Damage Reduction:
Max(20%, 4% * SkillLvl)

Attack MP Recovery Penalty:
-50%

Barrier Behavior:
- The barrier reduces damage once.
- After reducing damage, the barrier breaks.
- Certain attacks cannot have their damage reduced by the barrier.

Game Description:
Deploys a drone that creates a magic barrier. Reduces the power of straight-line, bullet, or bowling blade-wave attacks that pass through the barrier one time. The power of certain attacks cannot be reduced. While deployed, Attack MP Recovery is reduced.

SKILL: ATTACK BOOST
==================================================

Category: Golem Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 250

Source Details:
Type: Passive
Allowed Weapons:
- Bowgun
- Staff
- Main Magic Device

Description:
Improves the Magic Lamp's Attack allocation efficiency. Increases the attack-related benefit gained per Attack Point and always increases Golem Accuracy.

Golem Attack Multiplier Increase:
0.02 * SkillLvl * AttackPoint

Golem Accuracy Increase:
2 * SkillLvl^2

Golem Call Reactivation Effect:
Reactivating Golem Call while a golem is already on the field grants a one-time buff that increases the power of one Golem basic attack.

Lancer-Type Buffed Attack Multiplier:
GolemAttackMultiplier * (2 + 0.2 * SkillLvl)

Buster-Type Buffed Attack Multiplier:
GolemAttackMultiplier * (1 + 0.2 * SkillLvl)

Shielder-Type Buffed Attack Multiplier:
GolemAttackMultiplier * (2 + 0.2 * SkillLvl) + (0.4 * ShieldPoint)

Notes:
- The reactivation effect applies to one Golem basic attack after Golem Call is reactivated.
- The Golem Call reactivation bonus applies when using Bowgun, Staff, or Main Magic Device.

Game Description:
Improves the magic lamp to increase attack efficiency. Increases the boost received per lamp power distributed to Attack. Always increases Accuracy, no matter the distribution.

SKILL: BARRIER UPGRADE
==================================================

Category: Golem Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 250

Source Details:
Type: Passive
Allowed Weapons:
- Bowgun
- Shield

Description:
Improves Barrier Screen by increasing its damage reduction and reducing its Attack MP Recovery penalty.

Base Barrier Damage Reduction Increase:
SkillLvl%

Attack MP Recovery Penalty Mitigation:
2.5% * SkillLvl

Example:
At Barrier Screen Level 10 and Barrier Upgrade Level 10:
- Base Barrier Screen Attack MP Recovery penalty: -50%
- Barrier Upgrade mitigation: 25%
- Final Attack MP Recovery penalty: -25%

Game Description:
Improves the barrier drone. Boosts the barrier's damage reduction rate and lessens the decrease in Attack MP Recovery used to maintain the drone.
