==================================================
SKILL: PROCESS MATERIAL
==================================================

Category: Alchemy Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 25

Source Details:
Type: Utility

Description:
Allows the player to process items in the inventory and convert them into material points without spending Spina.

Material Point Result:
Items processed through this skill give material points at random, but provide at least:

Minimum Material Points =
(80 + SkillLvl)% of the normal material point amount

Notes:
- This skill's level is shared with Process Material in the Smith Skills tree.

Game Description:
Process materials by yourself. The success rate increases as the skill levels up. It is a common skill in Smith and Alchemy Skills.

==================================================

Category: Alchemy Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 25

Source Details:
Type: Crafting / Synthesis

Description:
Allows the player to create potions, ores, and gems without spending Spina.

Difficulty Limit:
SynthesisProficiencyLvl + TEC / 2 + TotalDEX / 6

Synthesis Success Rate:
(
SynthesisProficiency
+ TEC / 2
+ DEX / 6
+ INT / 10
- ItemDifficulty
+ MidClassSynthesisSkillLvl
+ HighClassSynthesisSkillLvl
+ ExpertsSynthesisSkillLvl

* (0.5 + 0.05 * ItemSynthesisSkillLvl)

You can synthesize items by yourself. The success rate is lower than the synthesists but you don't need Spina.

==================================================
SKILL: SYNTHESIZE EQUIPMENT
==================================================

Category: Alchemy Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: Level 50

Source Details:
Type: Equipment Synthesis

Description:
Allows the player to synthesize equipment characteristics without spending Spina or Orbs.

Equipment Requirements:
Two pieces of equipment of the same type are selected as synthesis materials.

Selectable Characteristics:
- Appearance
- Dyes
- Stats

Stats Characteristic Includes:
- ATK or DEF
- Weapon Stability
- Equipment stats
- Number of slots
- Crystas attached to slots
- Refine level
- Armor remodel

Notes:
- The individual properties inside the Stats characteristic cannot be selected separately.
- Unlocked characteristics are selected randomly between the two source equipment pieces.
- A characteristic can be locked to ensure the resulting equipment keeps it.
- The player starts with 1 available lock.

Additional Locks from Synthesis Proficiency:
- Level 50: +1 lock
- Level 100: +1 lock
- Level 150: +1 lock
- Level 200: +1 lock

Failure:
On failure, some locked characteristics may not take effect.

Equipment Synthesis Lock Penalty:
NumberOfLockedCharacteristics^2 / 2

Minimum Lock Penalty:
1

Equipment Synthesis Success Rate:
(50 + SkillEquipSynthesisBoost + 2 * TEC)
/
EquipSynthesisLockPenalty

Base Skill Equip Synthesis Boost:
0

Passive Skill Equip Synthesis Boost:
10 * SkillLvl

Game Description:
You can synthesize items by yourself. The success rate is lower than the synthesis but you don't need Spina.
