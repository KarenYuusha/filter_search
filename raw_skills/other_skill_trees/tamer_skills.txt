==================================================
SKILL: TAMING
==================================================

Category: Tamer Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Not specified in source
Type: Active / Capture

MP Cost: 300
Damage Type: None

Description:
Allows the player to capture a pet by using a Cage. A Cage must be present in the inventory.

Base Capture Values:

Timer:
SkillTimerBoost + MobNeutralProrationRate / 2

Maximum Taming Gauge:
10000 + 10 * MobPhysicalProrationRate

Mob Resistance Increase:
(10 + MobMagicProrationRate / 10) / 3

Mob Level Limit:
PlayerLvl - 10

Initial Taming Gauge:
0

Skill Timer Boost:
- Skill Levels 1 to 5: 25 seconds
- Skill Levels 6 to 10: 30 seconds

Mob Resistance:
- Starts at 0.
- Cannot go below 0.
- Cannot exceed 1100.

Mob Movement Effect:
- While the mob is moving, Mob Resistance continuously increases by MobResistanceIncrease.
- When the mob stops moving, Mob Resistance continuously decreases by 9 until it starts moving again.

Damage Interaction:
If the mob is not damaged during capture, Mob Resistance continuously decreases by 9 until the mob is damaged.

Source Uncertainty:
The source states that it is unknown whether damaging the mob completely stops this decrease or only applies a cooldown.

Base Taming Gauge Increase:
(1 - MobCurrentHP / MobMaxHP) * 30

Taming Gauge Increase or Decrease:
BaseTamingGaugeIncrease * (1000 - MobResistance) / 1000

Ailment Bonus:
Taming Gauge increase or decrease is doubled when the mob is affected by:
- Tumble
- Stun
- Sleep
- Stop
- Fatigue

Capture Success:
If the Taming Gauge reaches its maximum before the timer expires, the pet is captured.

Captured Pet Properties:
The captured pet receives randomly attributed:
- Weapon type
- Nature
- Potential base

The mob type determines its attack patterns.

Using a Captured Pet:
The player must:
1. Purchase land.
2. Release the pet from its Cage at the land.
3. Raise the pet.
4. Call the pet.

Capture Failure:
- The mob is inflicted with Rage.
- There is a chance to lose the Cage.
- Increasing Taming Skill Level decreases the chance of losing the Cage.
- Full effects are unknown in the source.

Capture Restrictions:
- Cannot capture a mob with Rage.
- Cannot capture a mob above the Mob Level Limit.

Passive Effect:
Mob Level Limit Increase =
SkillLvl

Game Description:
Capture the monsters weaker than you. The Max Lv of them and the time limit increase. The chance of breaking Cages decreases as the skill levels up. Consume Cage x1.

SKILL: PET HEAL
==================================================

Category: Tamer Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Not specified in source
Type: Active / Pet Support

MP Cost: 100

Description:
Restores HP to the player's currently called pet. Cannot be used on another player's pet.

Base Charge Time:
- Skill Levels 1 to 3: 6 seconds
- Skill Levels 4 to 6: 5 seconds
- Skill Levels 7 to 9: 4 seconds
- Skill Level 10: 3 seconds

HP Heal Percentage:
- Skill Level 1: 0.02
- Skill Level 2: 0.05
- Skill Level 3: 0.07
- Skill Level 4: 0.10
- Skill Level 5: 0.12
- Skill Level 6: 0.15
- Skill Level 7: 0.17
- Skill Level 8: 0.20
- Skill Level 9: 0.22
- Skill Level 10: 0.25

HP Heal Formula:
PetMaxHP * HPHealPercentage + 100 * SkillLvl

Bow Bonus:
- HP Heal Percentage: +0.15
- Charge Time: Halved

Staff Bonus:
- HP Heal Percentage: +0.15
- Charge Time: Divided by 1.5

Combo Restriction:
If used in a combo while no pet is called, the skill does not activate.

Game Description:
Restore the called pet's HP. Unavailable on other players' pets.
