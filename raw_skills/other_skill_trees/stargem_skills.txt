SKILL: RETRO BOWGUN
==================================================

Category: Stargem Skills
Tier: Not specified in source
Required Level: Not specified in source

Source Details:
Source: 2024 Halloween Stargem
Type: Active
Allowed Weapons: All Weapons

MP Cost: 100
Damage Type: Physical
Element: Fixed Neutral
Hit Count: 1
Maximum Cast Range: 12 m

Description:
Attacks the target and generates additional Aggro based on distance. Greater distance produces more Aggro.

Base Skill Multiplier:
0.5 + 0.05 * SkillLvl + DEX / 250

Base Skill Constant:
100

Additional Aggro by Distance:
- 0 to 5 m: 0 Aggro
- 6 to 8 m: 250 * SkillLvl Aggro
- 9 to 12 m: 750 * SkillLvl Aggro

Aggro Notes:
- These values use actual Aggro units and do not include base MP-cost Aggro.
- The source states that the final value gains 1000 additional Aggro from the 100 MP base cost.
- Only the Aggro holder gains the additional distance-based Aggro.
- Battle initiation is included for the Aggro holder.
- A non-Aggro holder generates only the MP-cost base Aggro.
- Additional Aggro is affected by Aggro+% equipment and buffs.

Range Modifiers:
Affected by both Short Range Damage and Long Range Damage.

Proration:
- Uses Physical Proration.
- Deals Physical Proration.

Motion:
Fixed Motion Speed.

Game Description:
A bowgun that is not very powerful and is mainly for display. Attacks the target to pull aggro. The greater the distance, the greater the effect. The distance thresholds are 6 m or more and 9 m or more.
