Category: Support Skills

GENERAL INFORMATION

Tier Requirements:
- Tier I: Level 10
- Tier II: Level 30
- Tier III: Level 70
- Tier IV: Level 150

Shared Rules and Notes:
- Support skills as the name suggests are various party buffer/healing skills to help support the party.
- All support skills except Sanctuary are un-comboable.
- The bottom 2 branches all have a nerf applied on the caster and disappear if the caster is damaged.
- The positive stats are applied to the whole party inside the aura including the caster.
- The bottom 2 branches support skills are all aura based support skills which generate [30 + 30 * PlayersConnected] amount of aggro per 3 seconds per active aura on all enemies having base aggro on you.
- Aura aggro is unaffected by Aggro+% equipment.
- Source note: using Asobimo aggro, 1 MP = 10 Aggro.


==================================================
SKILL: FIRST AID
==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 10

Source Details:
Type: Party healer
Action Range: 2m

Description:
Reduce the revive time for dead party members.

Revive Time Reduction:
30 + 2 * Lvl % of current revive time

MP Cost:
- Starts from 1 MP.
- Increases by 1 MP every time you use it consecutively.
- Reduced by 1 MP every 1 minute if no First Aid was given.
- Resets back to 1 MP by changing maps.

Notes:
- Click on a dead party member's HP bar or the character in the over world to drop a menu for giving first aid.
- Without getting this skill revive time reduction for giving first aid will be 30% of current time fixed.
- Giving first aid counts as party buffer aggro generation.

Game Description:
"First Aid" effect in Support menu increases. The waiting time to revive is largely reduced.

==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 10

Source Details:
Type: Party/Self healer

Base Cast Time: 1 second

Description:
Heal a small amount of the target character's HP. Reduce the revive time if they are dead.

30 * Lvl + 1 * Lvl% of target's MaxHP

- If used on self it acts as a self buffer aggro generator.
- If used on a party member it acts as a party buffer aggro generator.

Game Description:
Restore the target's HP a little. Reduce the target's waiting time to revive if the target is knocked out.
Mini Heal will target the Caster automatically if no target is specified

SKILL: RECOVERY
==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: II
Required Level: Level 30

Source Details:
Type: Party/Self healer
MP Cost: 100 MP
Action Range: 48m (For healing party only)

Description:
Cast a spell to cure ailments. Prevent getting an ailment within a fixed duration if used on a character having no ailment.

Effect:
Heal ailment if any or give a buff that prevents getting an ailment once.

MP Recovery:
Lvl * Lvl MP

MP Recovery Condition:
Recover MP if an ailment is successfully removed or a given buff successfully prevents getting an ailment.

Buff Duration:
10 + 2 * Lvl seconds

Notes:
- Click on a party member's HP bar or click their over world sprite to use the spell on them.
- If used on self it acts as a self buffer aggro generator.
- If used on a party member it acts a party buffer aggro generator.
- Currently unknown which ailment gets priority to be removed in the case its casted on a char having more than 1 ailment.
- This skill can not heal or prevent FLINCH, TUMBLE, STUN or KNOCKBACK ailments.
- It also can not prevent MAGIC FLINCH (FLINCH caused when getting hit by a non ailment move while casting magic skills).

Game Description:
Remove a target's one status ailment. If there is no ailment to remove, it becomes a buff to remove one ailment at once for a few seconds. Restore the user's MP a little when succeeding in removing an ailment.
Recovery will target the Caster automatically if no target is specified


==================================================
SKILL: SANCTUARY
==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 70

Source Details:
Type: Support skill {Placed}
MP Cost: 400 MP

Description:
Generate a platform which reduces damage taken.

Effect:
Place a platform on the ground standing on which reduces damage taken depending on your MaxHP.

Condition:
It will only reduce the damage taken if the damage is less than a % of your MaxHP.

Platform Radius:
1 + Lvl / 3 m (From centre = caster position)

At Level 10:
Platform Radius = 4.33m

MaxHP Damage Threshold:
(5 + 0.5 * Lvl)% of your MaxHP

Damage Reduction:
(30 + [(Lvl - 1) / 3] * 20)%

Duration:
5 + Lvl seconds

Notes:
- This skill acts as a party buffer aggro generator when placed at a position having party members within platform radius.
- The damage reduction applies to any category of attack including fractionals as long as the condition is satisfied.

Game Description:
Create a sanctuary and protect allies. Reduce damage taken while in the area. The amount depends on each player's MaxHP. Ineffective against fatal damage.

SKILL: LIFE RECOVERY
==================================================

Category: Support Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 10

Source Details:
Type: Aura based party/self buffer
MP Cost: 300 MP
Aura Radius: 5 + Lvl / 2 m (From caster as centre)

Description:
Cast an aura to heal the party's HP.

Effect:
Generate an aura which gives a buff to heal HP of the party members inside the aura.

Caster Penalty:
Increase the damage taken.

HP Heal Amount:
10 + Lvl * 4 + TotalVITofCaster

HP Healing Interval:
Every 3 seconds while having the buff

Damage Taken Increase:
+(400 - Lvl * 20)% more damage

Damage Calculation Note:
Applied multiplicatively at the end of damage calculation after added with Magic Barrier bonus and Gem effect.

Duration on Caster:
15 minutes

Notes:
- All aura based support skills get removed if the caster is damaged (This includes 0 dmg from skills like Perfect Defense except Zantei).
- The aura will stay if the caster dodges or evades however.
- All aura based support skills apply positive buff to party inside aura including the caster however the nerf is only applied to caster.
- Party members can get the buff only if they stand inside the radius of aura.

Game Description:
Create an area that regenerates HP. The player's DEF decreases. The effect ends when the player take damage.
