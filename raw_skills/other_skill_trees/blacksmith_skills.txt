==================================================
SKILL: PROCESS MATERIAL
==================================================

Category: Blacksmith Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: 25
Type: Utility

Description:
Allows the player to process items in the inventory and convert them into material points without spending Spina.

Material Point Result:
Items processed through this skill give material points at random, but provide at least:

Minimum Material Points =
(80 + SkillLvl)% of the normal material point amount

Game Description:
Process materials by yourself. The success rate increases as the skill levels up. It is a common skill in Smith and Alchemy Skills.

Notes:
- This skill's level is shared with Process Material in the Alchemy Skills tree.

==================================================
SKILL: CREATE EQUIPMENT
==================================================

Category: Blacksmith Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: 25
Type: Crafting

Description:
Allows the player to create equipment without spending Spina.

Crafting Notes:
- Equipment crafted this way has a very low chance of having 2 slots.
- The exact chances of producing 1-slot or 2-slot equipment are unknown in the source.
- NPC Blacksmith crafting requires Spina and cannot produce 2-slot equipment.
- NPC Blacksmith crafting and player crafting use the same material requirements.
- Created equipment does not receive ordinary stats directly; instead it receives Potential.
- On success, resulting Potential depends on base Potential, player stat allocation, equipment type, number of slots, and certain skills.
- Full effects of all Potential modifiers are unknown in the source.
- On failure, the resulting equipment may have lower Potential than its base Potential.
- On success, equipment may have higher-than-normal base ATK or DEF and higher-than-normal Potential.

Difficulty Limit:
BlacksmithProficiencyLvl + TEC / 2 + TotalDEX / 6

Crafting Success Rate at Create Equipment Level 10:
10 + DifficultyLimit - EquipmentDifficulty + STR / 10

Source Qualification:
The source describes this success-rate formula as "supposedly" correct.

Game Description:
Create equipment by yourself. Create Equipment is required to attach stats.


==================================================
SKILL: CUSTOMIZE EQUIPMENT
==================================================

Category: Blacksmith Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: 25
Type: Equipment Stat Customization

Description:
Allows the player to add and modify stats on equipment that has Potential greater than 0.

Base Statting Slots:
3

Rules:
- If a statting slot is already occupied by a stat, the stat itself cannot be changed, but its value can be modified.
- Adding stats consumes equipment Potential.
- Adding negative stats restores some Potential.
- Adding multiple stats from the same category applies a penalty to Potential removed or added.

Statting Success Rate:
230 * PotentialAfterStatting / PotentialBeforeStatting
+ 100
+ SkillStattingBonusRate

Final-Step Potential Rule:
If PotentialBeforeStatting is lower than the equipment's Base Potential on the final statting step:

PotentialBeforeStatting = BasePotential

Potential Returned by Negative Stats:
(5 + TEC / 10)% of the stat's Potential cost

Skill Statting Bonus Rate Increase:
SkillLvl

Game Description:
Consume potential and attach stats to the equipment created by Create Equipment.
