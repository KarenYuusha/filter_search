SKILL: DUAL SWORD MASTERY
==================================================

Category: Dual Sword Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 30

Source Details:
Lv 1 Skill; One-Handed Sword/Dual Swords only (depends on effect)
Passive Effect:
- Allows a One-Handed Sword user to equip a One-Handed Sword on their sub weapon slot, making them a Dual Swords; the sub weapon slot sword has all its stats negated, except for its element
- A Dual Swords has its ATK gain changed:
Main ATK = (player's level + Main Weapon ATK + STR + AGI + DEX * 2) * ATK% + flat ATK
- A Dual Swords has its attack animation changed, taking into account a hidden stat "Sub ATK":
Sub Weapon ATK = Attack value of subhand sword * (1 + Weapon ATK%/100 + refine of weapon²/200) + refine of weapon + flat Weapon ATK
Sub ATK = (player's level + Sub Weapon ATK + STR + AGI * 3) * ATK% + flat ATK
Sub Base Damage = (Sub ATK + player's level - mob's level) * (100 - mob's Physical Resistance)/100
- Dual Swords skills calculate their base damage differently, taking into account their Sub ATK and another hidden stat "Subhand Stability":
Subhand Stability = subhand sword's Stability/2 + STR * 0.06 + AGI * 0.04 + Stability% from equipments/ava/etc.
Dual Swords Skill Base Damage = (Main ATK + Sub ATK * Subhand Stability + player's level - mob's level) * (100 - mob's Physical Resistance)/100
- For auto attacks, the damage calculation of each hand will use the element of the sword on said hand; for Dual Swords skills, all skills have the Dual Element attribute and use the subhand sword's element as the secondary element; if the skill doesn't have a fixed element, it will use both swords' elements instead
- Accuracy -(55 - 3 * Skill Level)%; Critical Rate -(55 - 3 * Skill Level)%; total AMPR is doubled; these effects only apply if you're a Dual Swords
Game Description: "You can equip 2 One-Handed Swords. Reduces the decline in Accuracy Rate and Critical Rate as this skill levels up."
A Dual Swords is still considered as a One-Handed Sword for some skills

SKILL: AERIAL CUT
==================================================

Category: Dual Sword Skills
Tier: V
Required Level: Level 240

Source Details:
Lv 5; Dual Sword only
MP Cost: 100
Damage Type: Physical
Element: Wind, but has the Dual Element attribute (gets extra element from subhand sword)

Base Skill Multiplier (Main Hits): 2.15 + Spinning Slash's Level * 0.085 + Floor(DEX/10)% * Skill Level
Base Skill Constant (Main Hits): 50 + Spinning Slash's Level * 5
Main Hit Count: 3 Hits; damage calculation is done once then spread evenly between the hits

Base Skill Multiplier (Additional Hit): 5 + Skill Level + BaseSTR/100
Base Skill Constant (Additional Hit): 20 * Skill Level
Additional Hit Count: 1 Hit

Maximum Cast Range: 12m

Main Hits Ailment: Blind
Main Hits Ailment Chance: 25% [Source note: uncertain whether this is the same as Spinning Slash's Blind chance]
Main Hits Ailment Duration: 10 seconds
Main Hits Ailment Resistance: None

Skill Effect:

* This skill is not treated as Unsheathe.
* Short Range Damage and Long Range Damage can affect this skill.
* Long Range skill can affect this skill.
* If Spinning Slash's Whirlwind is currently active, using Aerial Cut while moving forward will perform the last/additional hit at an additional cost of 200 MP.
* Performing the Additional Hit immediately ends Spinning Slash's Whirlwind.
* The additional MP consumption for the Additional Hit is unaffected by Combo Tags or MP Cost modifying effects.
* This skill has two different proration effects in one skill:

  * Main Hits inflict Magic Proration.
  * Additional Hit inflicts Physical Proration.
* Damage from both parts is still based on Physical Proration.

Additional Hit Critical Damage:
The Additional Hit gains a passive Critical Damage bonus depending on Twin Slash's Critical Damage buff level. Twin Slash does not need to be activated; only its acquired skill level matters.

If the target is not Blinded:
Aerial Cut Critical Damage Bonus = Floor(50% of Twin Slash's Critical Damage Bonus)

If the target is Blinded:
Aerial Cut Critical Damage Bonus = Floor(50% of Twin Slash's Critical Damage Bonus) * 2

Example:
Twin Slash Level 5 gives 75 Critical Damage bonus.
Floor(75 / 2) * 2 = 74 Critical Damage when the target is Blinded.

Note:
According to the source, Aerial Cut's Critical Damage bonus is not halved when the player's total Critical Damage exceeds 300%.

Example:
If Aerial Cut gives +37 Critical Damage below 300 total Critical Damage, it still gives +37 Critical Damage above 300.

Distance Penalty:
If Aerial Cut is used from 8m or farther, the Additional Hit receives a flat Critical Damage penalty for every meter starting from 8m.

Flat Critical Damage Reduction:
20 * MAX(0; Distance - 7)

The BaseSTR multiplier bonus of the Additional Hit is also reduced by 20% for every meter starting from 8m.

STR Multiplier Bonus Reduction:
-[20 * MAX(0; Distance - 7)]%

Example:
If Aerial Cut is used at 12m:

* Additional Hit Critical Damage: -100
* BaseSTR multiplier bonus is reduced by 100%, removing the STR multiplier bonus.

Distance is determined when Aerial Cut is initially used, not when the last/additional hit is triggered.

The flat Critical Damage reduction and STR multiplier bonus reduction are the same regardless of whether the target is Blinded.

Fun Fact:
Aerial Cut's Main Hit Base Skill Multiplier is based on the total multiplier of Spinning Slash's First Hit and 3 Whirlwind Hits.

Example:
Spinning Slash Level 10:
1.5 + 3 * 0.5 = 3 multiplier

Aerial Cut's Main Hit therefore has the same 3 multiplier before its additional DEX scaling.

The Registlet "Spinning Slash Compress", which adds +0.5 multiplier to each Spinning Slash hit, also increases Aerial Cut's Main Hit multiplier.

With 4 Spinning Slash hits:
4 * 0.5 = +2 multiplier

This changes the corresponding multiplier from 3 to 5.

At maximum level with the Registlet:
Aerial Cut Main Hit Multiplier becomes approximately:
5 + DEX/100

Source credits this interaction test to Toriye.

Game Description:
“A sword technique released without deploying Spinning Slash. Dual Element with (One-Handed Sword). While deploying Spinning Slash, press the button to move forward and the powerful additional attacks will be unleashed.”

The power of Aerial Cut depends on the acquired level of Spinning Slash.

The Critical Damage increase from the acquired Twin Slash applies to the Additional Hit and is further increased if the target is afflicted with Blind.
