SKILL: RAMPAGE
==================================================

Category: Blade Skills
Tier: III
Required Level: Level 70

Source Details:
Lv 3 Skill
One-Handed Sword/Two-Handed Sword only
MP Cost: 500
Damage Type: None

Increase First 10 Auto Attacks Skill Multiplier:
0.1 + 0.04 * Skill Level; total multiplier for all hits

Increase First 10 Auto Attacks Skill Constant:
10 * Skill Level; total constant for all hits

First 10 Auto Attacks Hit Count:
4; damage calculation is done once, then divided between the hits

First 10 Auto Attacks Hit Count (Dual Swords):
7; damage calculation is done once, then divided between the hits alongside the subhand damage

Final Blow Skill Multiplier (First 2 Hits):
0.5 + 0.05 * Skill Level; multiplier for each hit

Final Blow Skill Multiplier (Third Hit):
2.5 + 0.05 * Skill Level

Final Blow Skill Constant (All 3 Hits):
300 + 20 * Skill Level; constant for each hit

Final Blow Hit Count:
3 hits; damage calculation is done for each hit

Buff Effect:

* Increases the user's next 10 auto attacks' damage (Rampage Stack) as well as changing their hit count.
* Attack MP Recovery +(2.5 * Skill Level).
* On the 11th auto attack, deals a Final Blow that is treated as a Physical skill with the Perfect Aim attribute.
* During the Final Blow animation, the user gets 90% damage reduction regardless of Skill Level and weapon.
* THS Ogre Slash buff's 99% damage reduction overrides this 90% damage reduction.

Buff Duration:
11 auto attacks OR until afflicted with any ailment OR 10 minutes

One-Handed Sword Bonus:
First 10 Auto Attacks Skill Multiplier +(0.05 * Skill Level)

Two-Handed Sword Bonus:

* Final Blow Skill Multiplier (First 2 Hits) +1
* Final Blow Skill Multiplier (Third Hit) +3

Notes:

* This skill cannot be reused if the buff is already active.
* If this skill is in a combo and is not the first skill of that combo, the combo ends at the first skill if the Rampage buff is already active.
* The auto attack Skill Multiplier and Skill Constant changes only apply to the main hand of Dual Swords.
* Combo Tags do not affect Total Rampage damage.
* The Attack MP Recovery buff of this skill is not doubled on Dual Swords.
* The Final Blow is not affected by Sword Techniques.
* The Final Blow is not affected by the Motion Speed stat, but is affected by Freeze and Trigger Slash.
* Rampage Stack will not decrease if the auto attack is evaded or missed.
* Rampage deactivates Power Wave while the buff is active.

Game Description:
"Furiously and consecutively attack a target. Greatly enhance normal attacks 10 times and a strong attack will be activated after that. Unable to use redundantly."
