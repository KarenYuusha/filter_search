Constant: 500
Multiplier: 8 * (1 + ((0.375 + (Lvl * 0.0125)) * Charge))
Charge time: = 2.5 – 0.5 * Floor((Lvl-1)/3) seconds every 1 charge or stack
• If “Divine Rigid Body” has been acquired and “Annihilator” is active, you gain 1 charge each time you deal damage with a Crusher Skill.
• Charge stack will be reduced from 5/5 to 1/5 if unused for too long.
Losing Charge Time from 5>4 stack = 2 + (Lvl) seconds
Losing Charge Time except 5>4 stack = all fixed at 6 seconds
Aoe Radius: 2m
Hit Count: 1 hit
Skill Effect:
• Maximum charges are 6. (0/5 ; 1/5 ; 2/5 ; 3/5 ; 4/5 ; 5/5 stack)
• If you suffer from Ailment [Lethargy], then Goliath Punch stack becomes 1/5 charge.
• Affected by Short Range Damage
• If the attack cast has the same Combo Tag as the buff cast did, the Combo Tag of the attack cast will be ignored
• The Combo Multiplier carries over from the buff cast; this takes into account if the attack cast's Combo Tag is ignored; the total Combo Multiplier is calculated as follows:
CrossFire Total Combo Multiplier = Attack Cast Combo Multiplier + Buff Cast Combo Multiplier - 100
• Remember! Out of all combo tags, Only Bloodsucker(lifesteal) and Mind’s eye(always graze/no miss) tags cannot apply on buff(Charge) cast, but Bloodsucker after-effect(Spirit = boost dmg), Smite, Save and the rest can apply on buff cast.
Note: The charge animation of this skill is not affected by motion speed from character's status, but can use "swift" combo tag's motion speed modifier.

==================================================

Category: Crusher Skills
Tier: Not explicitly specified in source
Required Level: Not explicitly specified in source

Source Details:
Limitation: Knuckle and knuckle-sub only
MP cost: 300
Skill type: Active
Cast Range: Infinite
Buff Effect:
When you use this skill, Regenerate your HP based on your MaxHP% and recover at two intervals, the first is when you use this skill and the second is when you use any skill after this skill. However, the healing buff is reduced a lot if you use breathwork when there is an interval time. (Noticeable, when you spam breathwork = low heal)
First Heal Buff= (Skill Lvl + Annihilator’s Level)% of your MaxHP
Second Heal Buff = ((Skill Lvl + Annihilator’s Level)/2)% of your MaxHP
If you use this skill again before 30 seconds have passed, both heals will get weaker (Weaker Heal Penalty = divided by 100).
However, if “Divine Rigid Body” has been acquired and you successfully parry enemy's attacks using "God Hand", the healing effect will refresh to it's original value.
If you have Annihilator level 10, then Annihilator passively can make Breathwork gain buff that can halves mp cost of next skill, similar to how impact halves mp
Game Description: A technique to control your breathing quickly. Immediately restore your hp when activated. HP is also restored when the next skill activates. Breathwork resilient decline for a while.
Joji Fact:
You don't need to use the Annihilator active buff skill. You can still use Breathwork only and still get buff halves mp of next skill. Also, no need to max Breathwork(just stay at lv 5), since that buff is from Annihilator passive effect(must max annihilator).

SKILL: TERRABLAST
==================================================

Category: Crusher Skills
Tier: IV
Required Level: Level 250

Source Details:
Lv 4; Main Knuckle Only
MP Cost: 800
Base Skill Multiplier: 9 + 0.6 * Skill Level + MAX(BaseSTR; BaseAGI)/100
Base Skill Constant: 700 + 10 * Skill Level
Hit Range: 4m around the caster
Skill Effect:
If you have Annihilator buff and target has Armor Break ailment, then this skill will become Absolute Critical.
Recover (God Hand stack * 100) MP everytime you use this skill.
When using this skill, give a 10 seconds(cannot be overwritten) Barrier HP buff equal to the hp lost. While the Barrier buff is active, it can reduce your damage received by up to 90%. However, taking damage will deplete the Barrier HP (1Dmg : 1Barrier).
For example, you use this skill when you have 840 HP out of 1000 MaxHP, then this skill gives you 159(15%) Barrier HP. If you take 100 dmg, then you take 10 dmg instead of 1 dmg and Barrier HP from 159 become 59. After that, you take 240 dmg, then your barrier become zero, and you take (240 - 59) dmg.
Game Description: A technique to crush the earth and use the uplifted bedrock as a shield. Deals damage to your surroundings and gets a barrier based on the HP consumed for 10 seconds. (Can’t be overwritten). Critical is guaranteed if Annihilator is in effect and the target is inflicted with Armor Break.
