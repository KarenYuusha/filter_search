SKILL: AUTO-DEVICE
==================================================

Category: Sprite Skills
Tier: I
Required Level: Level 50

Source Details:
Lv 1; Main Magic Device Only
MP Cost: 100
Values of each attack:
- Base Skill Multiplier: 1; total multiplier of all hits
- Base Skill Constant: 0
- Hit Count: 1 hit for attacks within 8m; 3 hits for attacks from 9m+
Skill Effect:
- Grants a buff on yourself to automatically perform a magic auto attack to the target whenever you are not doing an action (e.g. casting skill or auto attack or you’re not interrupted).
- Magic auto attacks have a maximum range of 12m; no attack is dealt if you’re beyond that range.
- Every time this skill deals damage to the target, recover your mp by 100% of your Total AMPR. However, if your target is within 8m or more, then this skill’s AMPR will be set to (Skill Level * 5)%
- This skill follows the entire magic skill mechanic, such as Damage based on MATK, Magic Stability, Magic Critical Rate, Magic Critical Damage, etc. It inflicts Normal Auto Attack Proration and also uses said proration for its damage calculation, however.
- The damage of the attacks are affected by auto attack damage modifiers, but skill modifiers don’t affect it.
- The skill interval hit per second depends on your interval auto attack. However, if your target is within 8m or beyond, then (1.5 - 0.1 * Skill Level) second interval penalty of this skill will be applied. this skill is already fixed at +50% Motion speed, so Motion Speed doesn’t affect this interval.
- This skill never misses, and does not trigger additional melee/magic.
- This skill will stop attack/working if you get afflicted by the Silence ailment.
Game Description: A technique allowing magic device to assist attack. Performs a normal attack on target after being idle for 60 seconds. Efficiency reduced for long-range (8m or more).

==================================================

Category: Sprite Skills
Tier: V
Required Level: Level 300

Source Details:
T5; [Active] Main Magic Device Only
MP Cost: 400

Maximum Cast Range:
12m

Debuff:
Life Explosion

Debuff Duration:
5 seconds

Lebenglanz's Life Explosion:

Damage Type:
Magic; uses accumulated MP instead of MATK

Base Skill Multiplier:
10 + Skill Level

Base Skill Constant:
400

Accumulated MP Mechanic:

* Attacking skills used against a target affected by the "Life Explosion" debuff accumulate MP.
* The accumulated MP is used to fill the usual ATK/MATK portion of the damage calculation.
* MATK itself is not included in Lebenglanz's Life Explosion damage calculation.
* Multiple attacks can be used to accumulate MP before the explosion occurs.

Damage Reference Value from Accumulated MP:
Magic Device Weapon Attack * Accumulated MP/200

Accumulated MP Reset:
Reusing Lebenglanz before the explosion occurs resets the accumulated MP.

Life Explosion Effects:

* Cannot critically hit.
* Ignores Guard.
* Always uses the stronger element against the enemy.
* MP used by Arcane Strike counts as 0 MP.
* This skill affects Guild Raid allies.

Heal Amount and Cap:
(1000 * Skill Level)/Members Affected

Heal Range:
7m radius from the target

AMPR Buff:
1 * Skill Level

AMPR Buff Condition:
Only applies when attacking enemies affected by "Life Explosion".

Damage Modifier and Proration:

* Not affected by Short Range Damage/Long Range Damage.
* Not affected by Long Range Skill.
* Affected by Zero Stance Regislet.
* Not affected by Combo effects.
* Uses Magic Proration.
* Deals Magic Proration.
* Casting Lebenglanz itself does not inflict proration.

Magic Device Bonus:
"Life Bomb" debuff.

According to the Game Description:

* Boosts Attack MP Recovery for party members attacking the target affected by the debuff.
* Accumulates a portion of MP used when attacking.
* Deals damage based on accumulated MP.
* Restores HP of nearby party members when the explosion occurs.

Game Description:
"A forbidden technique that reverses the phase of life that is lost to create explosive power. Puts a "Life Bomb" on the target. It explodes and deals damage when the effect ends."

SKILL: CURSED ALTAR
==================================================

Category: Sprite Skills
Tier: V
Required Level: Level 300

Source Details:
T5; [Active] Main Magic Device Only
MP Cost: 600

Maximum Cast Range:
12m

Ailment:
Curse

Ailment Chance:
100%

Ailment Duration:
(2 + Skill Level) seconds

Skill Effect:
Successfully inflicting Curse with this skill applies the "Cursed Altar" debuff to the target.

During the debuff duration, the target continuously receives damage.

Cursed Altar Debuff Damage per Tick:

Against Boss/Miniboss:
INT(2% * Current HP * (1 - MAX(P.Res; M.Res)))

Against Common Monster:
INT(10% * Current HP * (1 - MAX(P.Res; M.Res)))

Damage Formula Notes:

* Current HP refers to the target's current HP.
* P.Res refers to the target's Physical Resistance.
* M.Res refers to the target's Magic Resistance.
* Each tick's damage is calculated separately.
* Therefore, each subsequent tick uses the target's remaining HP after the previous tick's damage has been subtracted.

Tick Count:
Skill Level

Cursed Altar Stability:
MIN(Character's Physical Stability/2; 50)

Regislet Interaction:
This skill is not affected by the "Role Model" Regislet.

Game Description:
"Devours the soul with a curse. Puts a "Curse" on the target. If successful, an effect that continuously deals damage to the target is additionally applied (damage is reduced against bosses)."
